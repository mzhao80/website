I'm a Researcher at [OpenAI](https://openai.com/), working on metrics for recursive self-improvement (RSI). I study how AI is changing the research process itself. I'm based in San Francisco, CA.

Previously, I worked on code mid-training, post-training, and evaluations for Gemini 3, 3.1, 3.5, 3.6, 3.7, and 3.8 at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/). Before that, I was on the model training team at [Windsurf](https://windsurf.com/).

I graduated *summa cum laude* and Phi Beta Kappa from Harvard College with an A.B. in Computer Science and Government, and also concurrently completed my A.M. in Statistics. On campus, I led [Harvard Computer Society](https://www.hcs.harvard.edu), organized study groups with visiting fellows from Congress and the Cabinet through the [Institute of Politics](https://www.iop.harvard.edu), and sang all four years with the [Harvard Glee Club](https://www.harvardgleeclub.org).

In college, I conducted research in computational social science, with an emphasis on statistical methods for better understanding Congress and elections. My senior thesis "Words Speak as Loudly as Actions: Deep Learning Methods for Stance-Based Ideal Points from Congressional Speeches," advised by [Prof. Ariel Procaccia](https://procaccia.info/) and [António Câmara](https://ascamara.github.io/) won the Thomas Temple Hoopes Prize for outstanding thesis and the Gerda Richards Crosby Prize for the highest-ranked student in Government (pre-print forthcoming).

With [Prof. Kosuke Imai](https://imai.fas.harvard.edu/) and the [ALARM Project](https://alarm-redist.org/), I studied redistricting reforms, gerrymandering, and electoral competition, and helped develop an [R package](https://alarm-redist.org/alarmdata/) for working with redistricting data. You can find this work on my [Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) page.

I'm always happy to meet new people, gain new experiences, and chat about big ideas and interesting tidbits in the world – feel free to send me an email at michaelzhao[at]alumni[dot]harvard[dot]edu, or find me on [X](https://x.com/michaelzhao_)!
